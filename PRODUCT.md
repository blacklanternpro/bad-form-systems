# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary user: an owner of a South West WA trade, civil, fabrication, or fleet business who is deciding whether to book a yard visit. Typical situation: the general ledger (Xero or MYOB) is fine; the week still lives in Excel, paper, checklists, forms, and logins that do not talk. The job on this site is to decide if BAD FORM Systems is worth a 20-minute walkthrough, not to evaluate an agency portfolio, and not to buy a scanner.

## Product Purpose

BAD FORM Systems audits the workflow and builds a custom job system this business owns: jobs, costing, plant, variations, certificates, and the ute. Success is a booked yard visit that turns into a working system the crew will actually use. Xero or MYOB stay the books.

## Positioning

This is not a docket scanner, a receipt photographer, a Xero plugin, or a shelf IMS (SimPRO / AroFlo / Tradify). Capture is one door in, not the offer. Lead with scattered work becoming one custom-owned system. The ledger stays because we do not replace accounting. We do not lead with "beside Xero."

Show up on the ground in the South West. Built and supported from Bunbury.

## Operating Context

Owners and crews work from yards, utes, cabs, and workshops across Bunbury and the broader South West. Daily reality includes paper, wholesale counter receipts, WhatsApp, Excel, pre-starts, and delayed invoicing. Accounting stays with the existing bookkeeper. Evaluation of this product happens as a yard or workshop walkthrough, not a slide deck.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Confirmed on the current site: marketing pages for the offer, and a contact form that only delivers mail when Resend or a mailto fallback is configured.

Ghost-tax calculator, docket-ingestion demo, and published price bands are retired from public chrome. They are not the product.

Undecided / not product facts: published price bands, delivery-day SLAs, named clients, sector “typical profiles,” and any percentage or dollar-drag statistics. Those originated in a draft dump and must stay marked draft until replaced with real figures.

## Brand Commitments

The product name is **BAD FORM Systems**. It is a working arm of BAD FORM, not a rename of the parent. `bad-form.pro` is the parent’s irreverent high-end core; that approach will not win this sector. Systems needs a look and voice that can get industrial and trade clients. Do not invent clients, testimonials, or metrics.

Logo lockups currently in `public/` (D1 plate) are real BAD FORM assets; whether Systems keeps, adapts, or replaces that plate is undecided and is not a visual brief.

Public copy never uses: instance, packs, modules, kernel, platform, Foundry, DAYBOOK, pipeline, SaaS, all-in-one, seamless, streamline, elevate, next-gen, digital transformation, synthetic. Say: your own job system, your name on it, add civil / fab / fleet, paper trail, docket, read clean, sent to review, draft, demo yard, kept current, supported from Bunbury.

The marketing homepage must make the service legible in the first viewport: scattered work, then a custom system this yard owns. Photographs are demo atmosphere, never a fake client, and never a product you buy off the page.

## Evidence on Hand

Real: parent-brand SVG lockup and favicon in `public/`; site copy and IA in `src/content/`; the product itself lives in TradieIMS `platform/` (separate repo). Root `frontend/` and `backend/` there are leftover legacy IMS and are not what we market.

Absent and must not be fabricated: real customer names, testimonials, case studies, press, conversion metrics, and verified pricing.

## Product Principles

1. Custom-owned, not tacked on. The offer is the job system this yard runs on, not a capture plugin on Xero.
2. The ledger stays. Any system that asks the bookkeeper to leave Xero or MYOB is off-brief. Drafts only.
3. The owner is the buyer. Speak to the person who can book a yard visit.
4. Field adoption is the product. If the crew will not use it in the ute, it does not ship.
5. Local presence is the sales motion. Evaluation happens at the yard.
6. Proof is earned. No invented clients, quotes, or numbers.
