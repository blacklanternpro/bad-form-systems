# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary user: an owner of a South West WA trade, civil, fabrication, or fleet business who is deciding whether to book a yard visit. Typical situation: the general ledger (Xero or MYOB) is fine; jobs, dockets, variations, and field hours still live on paper, spreadsheets, and utes. The job on this site is to decide if BAD FORM Systems is worth a 20-minute walkthrough, not to evaluate an agency portfolio.

## Product Purpose

BAD FORM Systems designs and builds custom operational systems that sit beside the accounting tool the business already trusts. Success is a booked yard visit that turns into a working field-to-ledger workflow the crew will actually use.

## Positioning

Keep Xero or MYOB. Build the missing operations layer (job/costing brain plus ute/cab capture: photo, voice, simple screens) around how this yard already works. Show up on the ground in the South West rather than selling a generic SaaS rip-and-replace.

## Operating Context

Owners and crews work from yards, utes, cabs, and workshops across Bunbury and the broader South West. Daily reality includes paper dockets, wholesale counter receipts, WhatsApp, Excel, pre-starts, and delayed invoicing. Accounting stays with the existing bookkeeper. Evaluation of this product happens as a yard or workshop walkthrough, not a slide deck.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Confirmed on the current site: marketing pages for the offer, a ghost-tax calculator (illustrative, not a claim), a docket-ingestion demo (fallback payload when no Gemini key), and a contact form that only delivers mail when Resend or a mailto fallback is configured.

Undecided / not product facts: published price bands, delivery-day SLAs, named clients, sector “typical profiles,” and any percentage or dollar-drag statistics currently in copy. Those originated in a draft dump and must stay marked draft until replaced with real figures.

## Brand Commitments

The product name is **BAD FORM Systems**. It is a working arm of BAD FORM, not a rename of the parent. `bad-form.pro` is the parent’s irreverent high-end core; that approach will not win this sector. Systems needs a look and voice that can get industrial and trade clients. Do not invent clients, testimonials, or metrics.

Logo lockups currently in `public/` (D1 plate) are real BAD FORM assets; whether Systems keeps, adapts, or replaces that plate is undecided and is not a visual brief.

The marketing homepage must make the service legible in the first viewport: a field phone or office job screen (synthetic demo, never a fake client) showing job/costing/capture beside Xero — not only a visual metaphor.

## Evidence on Hand

Real: parent-brand SVG lockup and favicon in `public/`; site copy and IA in `src/content/`; working calculator and docket demo as demonstrations of the mechanism, not as published results.

Absent and must not be fabricated: real customer names, testimonials, case studies, press, conversion metrics, and verified pricing.

## Product Principles

1. The ledger stays. Any system that asks the bookkeeper to leave Xero or MYOB is off-brief.
2. The owner is the buyer. Speak to the person who can book a yard visit.
3. Field adoption is the product. If the crew will not use it in the ute, it does not ship.
4. Local presence is the sales motion. Evaluation happens at the yard.
5. Proof is earned. No invented clients, quotes, or numbers.
