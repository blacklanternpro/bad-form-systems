# BAD FORM Systems — design system

Working arm of BAD FORM for South West WA trade, civil, fabrication, and fleet owners. Paper/docket world, not the parent’s irreverent high-end site. Buyer is deciding whether to book a yard visit.

## Palette

- Paper ground: `#efe6c8` (`brand-black` — cream, not black)
- Manila header: `#d8c48c`
- Ply pink lockup: `#e8b4b8`
- Ink: `#1c1814`
- Stamp yellow: `#f3d31a` (visit verb)
- Cobalt: `#2242c8` (accent, IMS, stamp text)
- Steel: `#5c5348`
- Sheet footer: `#f3e6c4`

## Type

- Barlow 600 uppercase for homepage titles (`.type-hero`)
- Courier Prime for docket lines and `.btn-text`
- Barlow Condensed extra-bold only on the **BAD FORM** lockup — never as a poster on photos

## Controls

- Primary: stamp yellow rectangle, 2px corners, ink border, cobalt condensed “BOOK A SITE VISIT”
- Text link: Courier uppercase, ply underline

## Imagery

- Cab still: `/images/hero-cab.webp` — original cab photograph with generic Capture filling the photographed glass. Synthetic demo, not a client.
- Hand still: `/images/field-hand.webp` — phone-centric crop of the sharp `pour` composite. Synthetic demo.
- Office still: `/images/office-ims.webp` — laptop jobs dashboard in a yard office. Synthetic demo.
- Phone and laptop IMS must stay readable. Do not CSS-mirror photos. Do not invent clients, testimonials, or metrics.

## Voice

Keep Xero or MYOB. Custom IMS + ute capture. Evaluation is a site visit in the South West. Headline: “Keep the ledger. Build the job system.”
