# Site overhaul: copy and sections

Supersedes [homepage-redesign.md](homepage-redesign.md) for story and copy. Visual tokens, stills, and stamp CTA stay.

## Positioning lock

This is not a docket scanner. Not a Xero plugin. Not a shelf IMS.

We sit in the yard, audit the work that lives in Excel, paper, checklists, forms, and logins that do not talk, and **build a custom system this business owns**. A small start is possible. The full build is jobs, costing, plant, variations, certificates, and the ute. Xero or MYOB stay the books.

Never lead with capture, receipts, Sunday night typing, or "beside Xero." That is how the site has already been misread as a tack-on. Capture is one door in, not the offer.

The Fable/Jake bar: custom workflow digitisation after a local audit. Not a concreter product you buy off the page. Not a receipt photographer. Not three surfaces of the same app.

## Copy laws

1. Anxiety map. The 2am problem is the whole week living in five places that do not talk, plus the fear of buying another unused login or a one-off that dies. Relief is a system built around this yard.
2. Kill brochure English. Banned: revolutionize, seamless, empower, unlock, next-generation, robust, streamline, unleash, instance, packs, modules, kernel, platform, Foundry, DAYBOOK, pipeline, SaaS, all-in-one, elevate, digital transformation, synthetic.
3. Rhythm. Fragments, then a sentence that proves we have sat in the ute. No em-dashes.
4. Micro-copy earns its space.

One conversion label sitewide: `Book a site visit`.
