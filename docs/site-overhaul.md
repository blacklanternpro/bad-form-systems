## Positioning lock

This is not a rented job app. Not a Xero plugin. Not a shelf IMS.

The company owns the job system as an operating asset: built for this yard, kept by this yard, added to when the work changes, supported from the South West. Xero or MYOB stay the books. Do not claim source-code IP transfer.

Never lead with capture or "beside Xero." Capture is one door in, not the offer.

## Copy laws

1. Anxiety map. The 2am problem is another unused login, or a one-off that dies. Relief is software this company owns, added to here.
2. Kill brochure English. Banned: revolutionize, seamless, empower, unlock, next-generation, robust, streamline, unleash, instance, packs, kernel, platform, Foundry, DAYBOOK, pipeline, SaaS, all-in-one, elevate, digital transformation, synthetic.
3. Rhythm. Fragments, then a sentence that proves we have sat in the ute. No em-dashes.
4. Micro-copy earns its space.

One conversion label sitewide: `Book a site visit`.
